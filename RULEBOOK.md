# OBSCURA Rulebook

*OBSCURA: a student Capture the Flag event by LEAD Society, TIET.*

Welcome, Agents. The Archive has been stolen. Mogambo is *khush*, and your mission is to recover the five relics, uncover the hidden truth, and stop him before the picture ends.

Read the following rules carefully before starting your hunt.

---

## I. The Hunt

### 1. One Laptop Per Team

- Each team plays on **one laptop only**. Log in with your assigned team account on that laptop.
- Do not log in from a second laptop, phone or tablet. Teammates gather around the same screen.
- Do not share your team account with anyone outside your registered team.

### 2. The 60-Minute Countdown

- The competition runs for 60 minutes under one shared global countdown.
- All teams compete within the same time window. Gates open the moment the clock starts.
- Manage your time carefully. Every second counts.
- The hunt ends when you close the final face-off or when the countdown reaches zero, whichever comes first. Follow event staff instructions at the end.

### 3. Five Acts, Fifteen Checkpoints

- OBSCURA consists of five Acts, each containing three checkpoints: fifteen in all.
- Each finished Act rewards your team with one unique relic, even if you missed checkpoints in it.
- Collect all five relics to unlock the final face-off against Mogambo.

### 4. How to Progress

1. **Start.** Log in with your team account and open **Challenges**. Everything unlocks when the clock starts.
2. **Open a gate.** Begin with Act 1, checkpoint 1.1. Each checkpoint has its own gate page: a story, the challenge, a data pack or built-in tools where needed, and two optional hints.
3. **Solve and submit.** Work out the answer as a team and submit your flag (see Submitting Flags).
4. **Close the checkpoint.** A checkpoint closes when you solve it, or when you use all three attempts on it. Its **Reel Card** then appears: a story clue that carries the plot forward and shows the way to the next gate. Read it carefully.
5. **Keep to the order.** Checkpoints open strictly one after another. You cannot skip ahead, and a closed checkpoint cannot be reopened.
6. **Earn relics.** Closing the third checkpoint of an Act awards that Act's relic. Find your relics on the Relics page.
7. **Face Mogambo.** After Act 5, with all five relics in hand, the final face-off opens. It is one last challenge, worth the most points.
8. **Finish.** Your run ends when you close the final face-off or the clock runs out. Points scored by then are your result.
- In case of any discrepancy, ask your mentor.

### 5. Submitting Flags

- The answer box already shows `OBSCURA{` and `}` for you. Type **only your answer** between the braces. Do not type `OBSCURA{}` yourself.
- Answers are case-insensitive. Spaces, underscores, hyphens and commas are ignored, so `Red Door`, `red door` and `RED-DOOR` are the same answer.
- Read challenge instructions carefully and give exactly what is asked for (the right numbers, words and order).
- **You get three attempts per question, including the final face-off.** A wrong answer uses one attempt. After the third wrong answer the checkpoint closes with zero points and the next one opens.
- Agree on the answer as a team and check its format before you submit. You will be asked to confirm.
- In case of any discrepancy, ask your mentor.

---

## II. Scoring and Hints

### 6. Points and Scoring

- Points grow with every act. A correct answer scores **20 points in Act 1, 40 in Act 2, 70 in Act 3, 100 in Act 4 and 120 in Act 5**. The final face-off is worth **150 points**.
- A wrong answer scores zero and uses one of your three attempts. There is no negative marking.
- Hints never cost points. They are paid for with coins (see Hints and Coins).
- The maximum score is 1,200 points.
- Ranking: more points first. On equal points, the team that reached its last correct answer earlier ranks higher. If that is also equal, the team with more coins left ranks higher.
- In case of any discrepancy, ask your mentor.

### 7. Hints and Coins

- Every team starts with **120 coins**.
- Each checkpoint has two optional hints, unlocked in order. On every checkpoint, **hint 1 costs 10 coins** and **hint 2 costs 15 coins**.
- Coins are spent the moment you reveal a hint, whether or not you then solve the checkpoint. When your coins run out, no more hints.
- 120 coins covers hint 1 on 12 checkpoints, or both hints on only 4. Spend them wisely: coins left over can break a tie.
- In case of any discrepancy, ask your mentor.

---

## III. Tools and Fair Play

### 8. No External Tools or AI

- No internet searches, other websites, apps, **AI assistants** or any other outside tools during the hunt. It is played on this site only.
- Where a checkpoint needs a tool, such as a decoder or a calculator, it is built into that checkpoint's page. Built-in tools are free and never cost coins.
- Pen and paper are allowed for working things out.
- **If any mentor finds a team cheating, that team will be disqualified.**

### 9. Stay in Full Screen

- Keep the site in full screen while the clock is running.
- **Getting out of full screen counts as a violation.** So does switching to another window, app or desktop (Alt+Tab, Task View, touchpad swipes).
- **After 3 violations your team is disqualified:** its timer stops and no flags or hints are accepted. Only event staff can restore access.
- You will see a warning each time. Press the button on it to return to full screen.

### 10. Team Collaboration

- Collaboration among members of the same registered team is encouraged.
- Sharing flags, hints, answers or solutions with other competing teams is prohibited.

### 11. Prohibited Activities

- Do not attack the CTF platform, its infrastructure, or other teams' accounts.
- Unauthorized access, exploitation, or disruption of competition infrastructure is prohibited.
- Brute-force attacks against the platform or challenge infrastructure are prohibited.
- Do not manipulate scores, alter game progress, or interfere with another team's participation.
- Any violation of these rules may result in disqualification at event staff discretion.

---

## IV. Event Protocol

### 12. Ask Your Mentor

- Have a doubt about a rule, the platform or how the event works? **Ask your mentor.**
- Mentors will not give answers or hints for challenges. Hints are available on the site for coins.

### 13. Technical Issues and Support

- Report technical issues to your mentor or event staff immediately.
- Event staff will assess reported issues and determine the appropriate resolution.
- A technical issue does not automatically pause or extend the global countdown.

### 14. Final Decisions

- Event staff and mentors may clarify or update these rules during the event.
- Decisions on disputes, scoring and disqualification are made by the organisers and are final.
